Ability: ConquerPlanet
	Name: #ABL_CONQUER_PLANET
	Description: #ABL_CONQUER_PLANET_DESC
	Icon: ActionBarIcons::1
	Hotkey: C

	Range: 80
	Target: targ = Object

	//Every heavenly body type (planet, asteroid belt, star/sun,
	//satellite/station) can be conquered, not just planets.
	Either(Either(Either(TargetFilterType(targ, Planet), TargetFilterType(targ, Asteroid)), TargetFilterType(targ, Star)), TargetFilterType(targ, Orbital))
	//No TargetFilterNotQuarantined: that's a planet-only plague mechanic,
	//not relevant to our simplified conquest flow, and it would reject
	//every non-planet target outright since it only recognizes planets.
	//Conquer is now the single way to take any body, neutral or
	//enemy-owned -- Colonize has been removed entirely, so there's no
	//longer a conflict between the two on a neutral target.
	Either(TargetFilterSpace(targ), TargetFilterOtherEmpire(targ))
	TargetFilterNotInCombat(targ)

	IsToggleTarget(targ, Channels = True)

	//Conquest channels for 8 real-time seconds, then the body (and any
	//buildings on it) transfers to the conquering empire.
	AfterChannel(targ, 8, TakeControl(), Clear = False)
